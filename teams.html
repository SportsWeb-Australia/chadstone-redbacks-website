Men’s, women’s and junior programs — all welcome.

Men’s Program

Senior men, all grades

Senior men’s lacrosse from social right through to State League, with a proud finals history — the club re-entered the Men’s State League competition in 2022. New and returning players always welcome.

Coordinators Jonathon Lowe & Nick Ryugo
Training Tue & Thu, 5–8pm · Percy Treyvaud Reserve

Women’s Program

All levels welcome

Our women play as Chadfield (combined with Caulfield Lacrosse) — B-Grade premiers in 2022, in their first season together. A genuine come-and-try culture where no one gets left behind, welcoming women of all ages and abilities.

Coordinators Amy Caruso & Rachael Brick
Training Tue & Thu, 5–8pm · Percy Treyvaud Reserve
Join & play

Junior Program

Boys & girls, all ages

From first stick to rep pathways — fun, fast and welcoming, with no experience needed. Boys play U12, U14 & U16; girls play U12, U14 & U17; Under 10s play Lacrosse Victoria lightning carnivals.

Junior Coordinator James Conheady · 0407 705 367
Training Tue & Thu, 5–8pm · Percy Treyvaud Reserve

Where & when we train

All teams train at Percy Treyvaud Reserve, Chadstone Rd, East Malvern, Tuesday & Thursday from 5–8pm. New and returning players are always welcome — just come down, or get in touch first.

Seniors: info@chadstonelacrosse.com.au · Juniors: James 0407 705 367
