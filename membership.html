Chadstone Lacrosse Club offers an exciting alternative to mainstream sport, combining the speed, skill and excitement of the ‘Fastest Game on Two Feet’ with a welcoming club culture where every player belongs. Through inclusive programs, lifelong friendships and pathways for all ages and abilities, we help players learn, compete and grow.

2026/2027 registration & club fees

There are two payments to make.

1

Lacrosse Victoria membership

Lacrosse Victoria requests that all players, volunteers, coaches and officials register — there is no cost involved.

Register with Lacrosse Victoria →
2

Chadstone Lacrosse Club fee

Paid directly to the club. Discounts are available for students and family multi-entries.
