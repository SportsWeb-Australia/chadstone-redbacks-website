What is lacrosse?

The fastest game on two feet — speed, skill, strategy and constant involvement.

The game

Speed, skill & constant involvement.

Lacrosse combines the speed of basketball, the strategy of soccer and the athleticism of football into one fast, free-flowing team sport. Players catch, throw, cradle, shoot and defend with a stick — every position matters, and everyone is involved.

It’s an Olympic sport again at LA28, with genuine state, national and international pathways for those who want them.
